## Ecommerce Delivery Intelligence Platform

### Category

Ecommerce · Backend Architecture · Production Systems

### Headline

# Delivery promises made dependable

### Challenge

A large ecommerce business needed one reliable source of truth for delivery estimates across products, warehouses, locations, couriers, holidays, manufacturing delays, stock status, and express-delivery rules.

The existing process produced delivery-date accuracy of only around 30%, creating customer confusion, avoidable support calls, and unnecessary operational cost.

### Delivery

A centralized delivery-estimation service combined operational data and business rules from courier, inventory, ecommerce, and warehouse systems.

The platform also introduced Redis-backed caching, administrative data uploads, version history, backup and restore, and automated out-of-stock alerts.

### Results

- Delivery-date accuracy improved from approximately 30% to 95%
- Customer calls reduced by approximately 10%
- Around INR 1 crore in annual cost savings
- Approximately 1.45 million requests supported during the Diwali period
- Peak traffic handled at roughly 199% above the previous month

### Capabilities

Backend architecture, API design, business-rule modelling, external integrations, caching, production scaling, observability, and long-term maintenance.

## Centralized Lead Capture and Processing Platform

### Category

Ecommerce · Integrations · High-Volume Systems

### Headline

# Fragmented lead capture, rebuilt as one dependable system

### Challenge

Lead capture relied on spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

The business needed a centralized platform that could collect lead activities from multiple touchpoints, retain owned activity records, enrich applicable data, and synchronize it across marketing, sales, and analytics platforms.

### Delivery

A centralized lead service replaced the spreadsheet-based middleware.

The system collected lead activities across the business, normalized destination payloads, resolved the nearest store through an internal pincode API when relevant, and handled custom activity-type workflows across internal and third-party systems.

### Results

- Lead-activity volume increased from 177,080 in November 2024, the last full pre-service month, to 410,087 in January 2025, the first full month with the service
- 626,470 lead activities and 3,611,900 requests sustained within 30 days
- Daily lead-activity volume reached approximately 2.2 times the last full pre-service month in the first full month with the service
- Automatic infrastructure scaling supported traffic spikes without permanently overprovisioning compute

### Capabilities

System integration, backend architecture, business workflows, data enrichment, workflow orchestration, cloud deployment, scaling, and production support.

## Horecah Payments and Notification Features

### Category

Freelance · Cross-Platform · Payments and Notifications

### Headline

# Payments and notification journeys across web, Android, and iOS

### Challenge

A new hospitality hiring product needed two release-critical capabilities to behave consistently across web, Android, and iOS.

### Delivery

During a one-month feature engagement, end-to-end Razorpay payments and automatic push-notification journeys were delivered through the shared React and Capacitor application, its Node.js backend, and Hasura/PostgreSQL events.

The Razorpay integration covered backend order creation, live client verification, server-side signature verification, and webhook reconciliation. A broken upstream Capacitor package was forked and modernized for Capacitor 6, including its TypeScript toolchain, distributable build, module format, and checkout option types.

Notification delivery used Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service, with device-token routing and destination-aware deep links designed to open the correct screen consistently across web, Android, and iOS.

### Results

- Razorpay payments and push notifications launched with the product's first production release
- One shared payment flow supported web, Android, and iOS
- Automatic notification journeys delivered through FCM and APNs
- Platform-specific package and deep-linking failures resolved before release

### Capabilities

Cross-platform feature delivery, payment integration, push notifications, backend APIs, Hasura events, deep linking, third-party package adaptation, and production testing.

# UI/UX Direction

## Visual Style

Use:

- Near-black or deep-charcoal background
- Slightly lighter surface layers
- Soft off-white primary text
- Muted cool-grey secondary text
- Low-contrast borders
- One restrained cold accent such as icy blue, steel blue, or desaturated cyan

Avoid:

- Bright gradients
- Neon cyberpunk styling
- Excessive shadows
- Decorative blobs
- Fake terminal aesthetics
- Code rain
- Heavy glassmorphism
- Multiple accent colours

## Typography

Use the centralized Segoe UI system-font stack throughout. Local development and production must resolve through the same declared stack; do not label a fallback face as Inter or load a separate Inter build in only one environment.

Typography should carry most of the visual identity.

## Layout

- Main container: approximately 1120–1200px
- Reading width: approximately 650–760px
- Clear vertical rhythm
- Strong alignment
- Spacious but not wasteful
- Responsive grids
- Subtle section boundaries

## Interaction Design

Allowed:

- Soft fade and upward entrance on first view
- Small hover transitions
- Button movement of 1–2px
- Link underline or colour transitions
- Project-card border or surface shifts
- Smooth anchor scrolling
- Reduced-motion support

Avoid:

- Scroll-jacking
- Parallax
- Cursor-following effects
- Magnetic buttons
- Constant moving backgrounds
- Long intro animations
- Anything that delays access to content

## Accessibility

Required:

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Proper colour contrast
- Descriptive links
- Form labels
- Reduced-motion support
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Alt text for meaningful images

# Final Standard

The site should not try to prove experimental visual-design skill.

It should prove the ability to design and build a polished, usable, technically credible web product with restraint, hierarchy, and judgment.

Every design decision should improve at least one of the following:

- Hierarchy
- Readability
- Comprehension
- Navigation
- Trust
- Interaction feedback

If it improves none of them, remove it.
